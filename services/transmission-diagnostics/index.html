Common symptoms

Describe exactly what the vehicle does

Delayed or hard shifts

The vehicle may hesitate before moving, engage with a bump, or shift more harshly than normal.

Slipping or flare

Engine speed may rise without a matching increase in vehicle speed during acceleration or a shift.

Leaks or warnings

Fluid under the vehicle, a warning light, unusual noise, or a burning smell can provide useful clues.

Assessment

Not every shifting concern means a rebuild

  • Review of symptoms, warning lights, and available diagnostic information.
  • Checks for visible leaks or accessible related concerns.
  • Consideration of electrical, sensor, control, driveline, or mechanical causes.
  • A clearer next step before authorizing major work.

Mobile or shop
