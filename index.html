Frequently asked questions

How do I request service?

Call or text 416-577-8237, or email jadensauthoanddetailing@gmail.com. Jaden will reply directly to confirm the details and appointment arrangements.

What information should I provide?

Include your vehicle’s year, make and model, your location, and the issue or service needed. A useful photo can also help explain the request.

Can every repair be completed at my location?

No. Routine maintenance and many common repairs may be suitable for mobile service. Larger, more involved, or paint-related jobs may require the Port Credit shop. Jaden confirms the right location before booking.

Where is the shop?

The shop is in Port Credit. Contact Jaden with the vehicle and service needed; he’ll confirm the appointment and shop location directly.
